TURKI OMAR HOLDING GROUP — OFFICIAL WEBSITE EXPERIENCE
Design the official website for Turki Omar Holding Group as an ultra-premium bilingual (Arabic-first) immersive storytelling experience.
This is NOT a traditional corporate website.
The website should feel like a cinematic digital manifestation of a visionary holding group that builds institutions, empowers innovation, and shapes the future.
The final result should blend:
	•	40% Apple elegance and simplicity.
	•	40% Awwwards-style immersive storytelling.
	•	20% luxury editorial sophistication.
Visitors should not feel like they are browsing a website.
They should feel like they are entering the world of Turki Omar Holding Group.

BRAND POSITIONING
Turki Omar Holding Group is a leading investment and operational group dedicated to establishing, scaling, and empowering innovative businesses through strategic leadership, digital transformation, advanced technologies, and long-term partnerships.

LOGO INTEGRATION
Use the official Turki Omar Holding Group logo throughout the website.
Do NOT redesign the logo.
